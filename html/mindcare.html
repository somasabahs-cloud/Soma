ROLE

UX/UI Designer

PROJECT

Mobile app

WORK

Design

OVERVIEW

Creating a supportive everyday experience.

MindCare is a mobile app concept designed around mental wellbeing and everyday support.

The concept combines different features into one simple experience.

THE CONCEPT

Small interactions for everyday use.

The app includes a daily check-in where users can reflect on how they are feeling.

Other features include mini-games, activities, an AI assistant and a garden that represents the user's progress.

DESIGN

Keeping the experience simple.

The interface was designed to make the different features feel connected and easy to understand.

The goal was to create an experience users could interact with without making it feel complicated.

TESTING

Improving the design through iteration.

The concept was tested and changed based on feedback.

Testing helped us understand which parts needed to be clearer and where the design could be improved.
