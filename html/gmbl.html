PROJECT

Internship

OVERVIEW

Making digital treatment easier to understand.

During my internship I worked with Gmbl Insight, a digital treatment platform for gambling addiction.

My work focused on improving the structure, creating clearer user flows and designing wireframes and prototypes for desktop and iPad.

MY ROLE

From structure to prototype.

I worked with the structure of the treatment platform, user flows, wireframes, prototypes and UI improvements.

I also worked with text changes and helped organise the treatment content into smaller and easier sections.

THE CHALLENGE

Too much information at once.

Some parts of the treatment contained a large amount of text and information.

This could make the experience overwhelming and make it more difficult for the user to understand what to do next.

DESIGN PROCESS

Breaking the experience into smaller steps.

The treatment modules were reworked into smaller focus parts to create a clearer structure.

I explored progress indicators, estimated time, smaller steps and options for showing more information when needed.

LEARNINGS

Designing around difficult content.

The project showed me how important structure and clear communication are when designing experiences with large amounts of information.

I also gained experience working remotely with designers, developers and stakeholders.
